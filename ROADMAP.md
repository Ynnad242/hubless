# Roadmap

What's planned for Hubless, roughly in order. Want something else, or own a device on this list?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/Ynnad242/hubless/issues).

## New kinds of devices

- **Webcams.** Settings for webcams such as the Logitech C920/C922 and Razer Kiyo: focus, exposure, white balance,
  zoom, brightness and the like, with presets.
- **Controllers.** Gamepads such as Xbox, PlayStation DualSense and 8BitDo controllers: battery, button remapping,
  stick dead zones and trigger settings where the controller supports them.

## Keyboards

- **Other Wooting keyboards** (Wooting 80HE, 60HE+, Two HE, UwU and older models): they speak the same protocol
  as the 60HE Hubless already supports, with different layouts.
- **Logitech G keyboards** (G915, G915 TKL, G PRO, G513, G815 and others): lighting, G-keys and game mode, over the
  same HID++ protocol Hubless already uses for Logitech mice.
- **Custom keyboards with VIA or Vial** (QMK firmware): key mapping across layers, macros and lighting for any
  board that supports VIA or Vial, like the browser configurators at usevia.app and get.vial.today.

## More mice

- **More Logitech G mice:** confirm the 61 models that should already work (G Pro, G Pro X Superlight, G502, G305,
  G703 and more) and add older and newer ones the app doesn't recognise yet.
- **Razer:** confirm the 74 Razer models on real hardware (send a device report from Hubless if you own one).
- **Lamzu and more WLmouse models** (Beast X, Strider, Sword X): they use the same protocol as the WLmouse Ying.
- **WLmouse Ying:** button remapping and its wireless 8K receiver.
- **Endgame Gear OP1 8k v2.**
